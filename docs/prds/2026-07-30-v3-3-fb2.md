# PRD — App 3.3.0-beta (founder feedback FB2, round 3)
Date: 2026-07-30 · Label: [DECISION founder priorities + AI-GEN spec] · Origin: `FB2.docx`, founder, tested against v3.0

**Rule 4 honesty note:** the spec for this build is the triage document `docs/qa/2026-07-30-founder-feedback-fb2.md`, written before any code was touched — it carries the per-item root causes and status. This PRD records the decisions and boundaries rather than restating it.

## The problem
Nineteen observations from a real session on the founder's phone. Three complications the raw list doesn't show:
1. **It was written against v3.0.** 3.1 and 3.2 shipped after. Five items were already fixed — building them again would have wasted the round and produced a false "fixed" signal.
2. **One item was misdiagnosed by its own symptom.** "The age toggle doesn't work" looks like broken state; it was a render truncation. Fixing the toggle would have changed nothing.
3. **One item is architecturally impossible right now** (multi-parent accounts) and needed a straight answer, not a partial build.

## Founder decisions taken this round
| Decision | Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Rating scale | **Five fresh tiers** (over "keep 3 as anchors") | Loved it · Liked it · It was okay · Not great · Not today. Founder accepted the migration judgment call; mapping chosen to be lossless at the anchors — `fine→okay`, since both are the neutral middle |
| Gender | **Girl / Boy / Prefer not to say** | Drives she/he/they. Unset — including all existing profiles — gets they/them |
| Build | **Install Node** | Unblocked a testable build; also a Phase 2 prerequisite |

## Building
- Five-tier ratings with a one-time lossless migration; category scoring moves from per-key counts to summed weights so a future tier needs no new counter.
- Gender field + a full gendered-copy sweep of `app.jsx` **and** the 155 activity blurbs in `data.js`.
- Browse: two separate age lists so the "coming later" set can't be cut by the 60-card cap; toggle moved to the top.
- Back-to-top on all lists; tab switches reset scroll.
- Sticky save action in the check-in sheet.
- Real place search (`PlaceInput`) in check-in and custom memories, seeded with previously-logged places.
- Favourites (⭐) with a filter; Memories filters for each rating tier and for "we did on our own".
- One shared marked picture for user-added places.

## Explicitly NOT building
- **Multi-parent accounts (FB2-17).** Device-local app, no server, no auth. Needs the Phase 2 Supabase stack and is gated by D3 compliance. A local-storage-backed "accounts" screen would imply sharing that does not exist — worse than nothing.
- **Venue-level place accuracy.** The geocoder returns addresses, not "the good playground". Phase 4 Places, unchanged.
- **Rebuilding the five items already fixed in 3.1/3.2.** They get re-tested (Part A of the QA script), not rewritten.

## Signal discipline (ADR-0005 / ADR-0007 check)
Ratings remain the **only** heavy signal. Favourites are the parent's keepsake mark and are excluded from `score()`, exactly as journal entries and swipes are. Adding two rating tiers deepens the existing signal; it does not add a new one.

## Design check (ADR-0005 — no guilt)
Five tiers risk turning a day out into a test score. Mitigations: the wording stays conversational ("It was okay", "Not great") rather than numeric; five stars was explicitly offered and rejected for this reason; "Not today" keeps its original blameless framing at the bottom of the scale.
