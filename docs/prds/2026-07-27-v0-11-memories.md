# PRD: v0.11 — Memories as the hook (filters, search, photo grid, journal)
Target: App 0.11.0 · [ASSUMPTION, ADR-0009] · Origin: founder — Memories should be informative and browsable, the surface that hooks.
## Done means
- Stats header: outings · unique places · photo count · since-date.
- Story/Photos view toggle; Photos = 3-col grid of all (filtered) photos, tap → full-screen lightbox with caption; thumbnails in Story also open lightbox.
- Filters: 😍 Loved, 📷 With photos (toggleable), plus free-text search over place/name/note.
- ✍️ Write a moment: freeform journal entries (text and/or up to 6 photos), styled distinctly (marigold spine), persisted like visits.
- Signal discipline: journal entries NEVER feed the recommendation engine (parent voice ≠ child response); excluded from pending-check-in logic.
- Insights panel shown in Story view only.
## NOT building
Editing/deleting entries (next cycle); date-range filters; export (Phase 2+); video.
