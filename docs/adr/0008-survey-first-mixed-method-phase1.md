# ADR-0008: Phase 1 shifts to survey-first mixed method (survey breadth + interview depth)
Date: 2026-07-27 · Status: Accepted · Amends: Phase 1 method in ROADMAP (interview-only)
## Context
Founder's personal network contains few caregivers of 6mo–3y children; recruiting 10 warm interviews is not feasible. Pure surveys, however, carry two known validity risks: self-selection (pain-havers over-respond) and prompted-answer bias (questions suggest pains).
## Decision
Mixed method: (1) an anonymous ~5-min public survey (FB groups, Reddit) with the open-ended "last Saturday" question placed FIRST, before any pain-themed content, coded against a pre-registered rubric — the survey equivalent of "unprompted"; (2) the survey's final question recruits volunteers for ≥5 stranger interviews ($10 gift-card thank-you) using the existing interview guide. Recruiting copy must be neutral and explicitly invite contented-routine parents. Gate math amended in ROADMAP with conservative thresholds acknowledging self-selection.
## Consequences
Larger, colder sample (better than 10 friends being nice); weaker per-respondent depth compensated by funneled interviews; analysis rubric pre-registered in the survey spec so results can't be reinterpreted after the fact. Survey responses are [EVIDENCE]; the CSV export is filed in research/notes/ on collection close.
