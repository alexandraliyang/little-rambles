# ADR-0005: Science framed as opportunity, never judgment (anti-guilt-marketing)
Date: 2026-07-27 · Status: Accepted — values decision, recorded before growth pressure could test it
## Context
The same CDC milestone data powering our engine powers anxiety-marketing apps ("is your baby behind?"). Honest science also says nearly everything is enriching for a 1-year-old. The anxious version of this product would likely convert better.
## Decision
Structural rules, not tone: the map stores emerging skills (opportunities), never expected achievements (judgments); no child-to-child comparison anywhere including marketing; "the library again is genuinely fine" is said out loud; marketing copy audited against this ADR before launch (ROADMAP Phase 5).
## Consequences
A weaker sales pitch, accepted knowingly. Trust becomes the brand. Any future feature or copy that manufactures developmental anxiety requires superseding this ADR in writing — which is the point of writing it down.
