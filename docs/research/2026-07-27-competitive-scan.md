## Headline verdict
The three-way combination — developmental-stage matching + local outing discovery + near-zero-friction logging/journal — **does not exist in any scaled product as of mid-2026.** The market splits into at-home developmental apps (Lovevery, Kinedu, BabySparks, Huckleberry) that never touch local venues, and local discovery directories/blogs (Winnie legacy, Tinybeans editorial, VancouverMom) that never do stage matching. Only sub-scale probes exist (OdekakeAI JP, Family Outing AI, one US county municipal app). Whitespace confirmed; the graveyard explains why it's unclaimed, not that it's unclaimable.

## The graveyard and its causes
- **Hoop (UK):** 1.5M+ families, £2.4M raised, 10% booking commission → shut down July 2020 when lockdown took booking revenue "to near zero." Relaunched 2023 under new owners; age tags now a commodity feature. Causes: events-DB maintenance burden + in-person-revenue fragility.
- **Winnie (US):** $15.5M raised for kid-friendly-places discovery → abandoned it; pivoted to a childcare marketplace (real transaction, real money). Cause: discovery alone wasn't a business.
- **Red Tricycle:** 20M+ monthly users, ad-monetized → acquired by Tinybeans (2020, $6.5M + stock). Cause: ad model needs enormous traffic; exited rather than scaled.
- **KidPass:** class-pass membership → pivoted to online classes/tutoring → acquired. **Sawyer:** survived by becoming B2B SaaS for activity providers — the durable money was supply-side software.
- Named recurring killers: **events-database tar pit · two-sided cold start · thin monetization of discovery · external-shock fragility · age-out churn.**

## What parents pay for (and don't)
Proven paid pains: sleep (Huckleberry Plus ~$11.99/mo, Premium $14.99/mo), development anxiety/activities (Kinedu ~$7.50/mo–$119.99/yr; BabySparks $4.99–19.99/mo; Lovevery kits $80–120 — **note: Lovevery does not ship kits to Canada**), memory-keeping (Tinybeans $7.99/mo/$74.99/yr, ~90k paid post-Qeepsake, ~US$8.9M rev; Qeepsake ~$47.88–95.99/yr, ~50k US paid at acquisition). NOT paid for: "ideas" — free AI is the default substitute (79% of US parents have used AI, 29% daily — Menlo Ventures 2025; 52.7% of parents use ChatGPT for parenting purposes — CHI 2024).

## Subscription benchmarks (RevenueCat 2025/2026, cross-category, directional)
Freemium converts a median ~2.1% of downloads to paid in 35 days; hard paywalls ~10.7% (but similar year-1 retention). 82–85% of trial cancellations happen day 0 → activation must land in session one. ~30% of annual subscribers cancel in month 1. Annual plans (60–70% of mix when pre-selected) improve LTV. Incumbent gravity: pre-2020 apps take 69% of subscription revenue; 2025+ launches ~3%.

## Vancouver context
No app-based incumbent — blogs (VancouverMom.ca) and web directories (Let's Play Inside) only. Easier field, but limited proven local willingness-to-pay; scan recommends treating Vancouver as validation/beta metro with a larger second metro (Toronto/Seattle) for monetization proof.

## Strategic implications adopted into ROADMAP (2026-07-27)
1. Launch on **durable venues only** (parks, pools, libraries, aquarium, farms, indoor play) — never an events calendar; tap-to-go logs crowdsource freshness (tar pit → data flywheel).
2. **Discovery stays free** (acquisition hook); monetize the shock-resistant, AI-proof assets: journal/keepsake export, preference-learning, verified "open now / stage-fit."
3. Price $6–8/mo with **annual pre-selected (~$50–60/yr)**; benchmark trial→paid ≥8–10%, month-1 retention ≥40%.
4. New watch-risk: **whitespace closure** — Tinybeans (Red Tricycle legacy + Qeepsake) or funded AI-family players (Ohai ~$9.99–19.99/mo, Joy ~$14M raised) shipping stage-matched local discovery. Review quarterly.
5. Age-out defense: multi-child support + preschool extension + keepsake endgame (exportable book).
